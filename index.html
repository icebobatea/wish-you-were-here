Wish You Were Here

Before phones had cameras, a postcard was how you showed someone where you were. Museums Victoria keeps nearly a thousand of them — souvenirs of a grand exhibition, notes from soldiers in the desert, and messages from migrants to the families they left behind. Here is what they tell us.

Where the postcards take us

Most of the cards picture Australia, and most of those picture one building: Melbourne's Royal Exhibition Building. But a second cluster sits half a world away, in Egypt and Iraq. These are the postcards Australian soldiers bought and sent home during the First World War.

The golden age of the postcard

About seven in ten dated cards in the collection come from just twenty years. Between 1900 and 1919, cheap printing and a reliable postal service made the postcard the text message of its day.
